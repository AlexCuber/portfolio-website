    (02) About

    The human behind the models

    Data scientist by day, builder of small machines by night.

    I like signals other people throw away.

    By day I wrangle manufacturing data, train cost-prediction models and teach wearables to understand movement. The common thread: taking something noisy and real (factory floors, bank accounts, ankle sensors, the air in my room) and making it make sense.

    Away from the keyboard I'm usually exploring: hiking somewhere green, hunting down light installations after dark, or adding one more specimen to a slowly growing mineral collection. I just like building stuff: software, circuits, furniture, whatever's in reach.
